Our Mission

Helping Hands Foundation is a non-governmental organization dedicated to improving people's lives. Our mission is to provide education, healthcare, food and basic facilities to underprivileged communities. We believe everyone deserves a better future.

Our Ongoing Projects

Education for All

Providing free education, books and school supplies to children in need.

Food Distribution

Distributing healthy meals to poor and homeless families.

Environment Protection

Organizing tree plantation drives and cleanliness campaigns.

Become a Volunteer

We welcome people who want to contribute their time and skills to society. Volunteers can participate in teaching, food distribution, awareness campaigns and environmental activities.
